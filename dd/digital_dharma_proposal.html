01 • The Problem

Threats Happen at Home, Not in IT Labs

  • Shared Device Exposure: 500M+ Indian kids browse YouTube, gaming apps, and WhatsApp on family phones without supervision.
  • The Language Barrier: High-risk threats like Digital Arrest scams, OTP fraud, and APK malware are unintelligible in English.
  • Voice-First Reality: Young children cannot type fast in Indian scripts; when frightened, their natural reaction is to speak.
🚨 Existing tools are heavy, English-only, and require logins—leaving vernacular households completely defenseless.
