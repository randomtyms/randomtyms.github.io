Gateway city · Southern Railway

1. Chennai Central — The Journey Begins

The gateway where our dynamic AI sibling duo, Lokesh and Varsha, discover the magic ticket. Crafted using advanced text-to-video generative models to preserve the iconic red-brick architecture of Namma Chennai rather than a generic transit hub.

Did you know? Chennai Central opened in 1873, and its Romanesque red-brick building is still one of the city's best-known landmarks.

Ask your child: Why do you think railway stations were built as grand buildings back then?

Meenakshi Amman · Majestic gopurams

2. Madurai — Majestic Gopurams & Traditions

The first destination stamp brings the towering Meenakshi Amman Temple to life. The AI environment models the historic layers of the gopuram structure, serving as a backdrop for Varsha's curiosity while exploring authentic Madurai street food.

Did you know? The temple complex has 14 gopurams, and the tallest, the south tower, rises about 170 feet.

Ask your child: Why are the gopuram figures painted in so many colours? What stories can you spot?

Now Madurai Short ↗

Brihadisvara · Raja Raja Chola I · c. 1010 CE

3. Thanjavur — 1000 Years of Engineering Marvels

Focusing on Raja Raja Chola I’s architectural masterpiece, the Brihadeeswarar Temple. Lokesh explores interlocking stone masonry and inclined physics, converting an ancient architectural marvel into an engaging modern STEM lesson.

Did you know? The temple is part of the UNESCO World Heritage site "Great Living Chola Temples", and its granite tower is about 66 metres tall.

Ask your child: How could builders lift huge stones to the top without cranes? Think about ramps.

Thanjavur Short ↗

Land’s end · three seas · Vivekananda Rock

4. Kanyakumari — Where Three Seas Meet

Reaching the southern tip where the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, and Indian Ocean meet, with Vivekananda Rock Memorial offshore. Known for its sunrise and sunset views over the water.

Did you know? Nearby stands a 133-foot statue of Thiruvalluvar, one foot for each of the 133 chapters of the Thirukkural.

Ask your child: Which Thirukkural do you know? What does it teach us?

Kanyakumari Short ↗

UNESCO World Heritage · Behind-the-Scenes Reality

5. Nilgiri — Cinematic Reality Check

A UNESCO World Heritage steam train journey through lush tea gardens turns into a playful lesson. When Amma delivers a firm rail safety reality check, it results in a comical 100 thoppukaranams penalty, showing how AI portrays relatable culture.

Did you know? The Nilgiri Mountain Railway opened in 1908, uses a rack-and-pinion system to climb steep slopes, and became a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2005.

Ask your child: Why must we never stand near the track edge or lean out of a moving train?
